How to play

SPIN puts your bet on row 1 and spins its 4 reels. Each reel stops on a blank, a 💣 bomb or a door.

💣 Any bomb busts the row and you lose everything riding on it.

If there's no bomb, every door opens to a gold coin. Coin prizes are based on the amount riding on the row, so the more you have riding, the bigger the coins. Your POT is your bet plus every coin won so far.

PRESS (free) lets the whole pot ride on the next row, so its doors pay off the bigger amount. A bomb loses it all.

SPIN after a win collects the pot and starts a new game. The pot is also collected automatically after row 4.

MAX PRESS lets your bet ride through all 4 rows automatically.
